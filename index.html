The idea

A navigator that doesn't say "wrong turn" — it just quietly recalculates.

Steply looks at what you have, what's realistic, and tells you what actually matters right now. No planning sessions. No setup ritual. Just talk — and get clarity in seconds.

How it works

Four things that make it different

01 — Just talk
[ listening screen ]

Say it on the go

Between clients, in the car, after a call. Steply hears what's on your plate and turns it into a structured plan — no typing needed.

02 — Priority out of chaos
[ plan screen ]

Top 3 that actually fit

12 tasks, 4 hours left. Steply picks what realistically fits today — and moves everything else to tomorrow, not the trash.

03 — One thing right now
[ single task screen ]

Can't decide? Don't.

Ask Steply to pick the single most important task. One answer, one reason — and you start moving.

04 — Knows your context
[ onboarding screen ]

Connects to your apps

Notion, Todoist, Google Calendar — Steply reads what's already there. No re-entering anything. Or just start fresh by telling it what's on your mind.
